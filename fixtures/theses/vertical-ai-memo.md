# Meridian Ventures: Vertical AI Thesis Memo

We back seed and Series A companies applying AI to regulated, workflow-heavy
industries: healthcare operations, financial compliance, insurance, and legal.

The market we underwrite must be at least $1B TAM with a credible bottoms-up
path; we discount top-down analyst figures. We want products embedded in a
daily workflow, not analytics dashboards, and we expect evidence of usage
depth: daily active seats, retention above 85% at six months, and expansion
inside existing accounts.

On teams, we require domain expertise: at least one founder who has operated
inside the target industry, plus in-house ML capability. First-time founders
are fine when the domain depth is real. We avoid teams outsourcing core
engineering.

Traction bar at seed: $250K ARR or three enterprise design partners with
signed pilots. At Series A: $1.5M ARR growing 3x year over year, with gross
margins above 65% and a clear services-to-software ratio below 20%.

Deal breakers: consumer social, pre-revenue hardware, crypto trading
platforms, and any company whose data rights are not contractually secured.
Round sizes we lead: $1.5M to $10M for 10-20% ownership.
