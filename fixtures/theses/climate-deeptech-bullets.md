# Climate Deeptech Screening Criteria

- Stage: pre-seed and seed only; first institutional check
- Sectors: industrial decarbonization, grid software, storage chemistry, carbon measurement
- Market: $5B+ addressable by 2032; regulatory tailwind identifiable (IRA, CBAM, EU ETS)
- Technology: defensible IP or process advantage; lab-validated at minimum, pilot-ready preferred
- Team: technical founder with published work or patents in the field; commercial cofounder preferred
- Traction: LOI or paid pilot with an industrial partner counts more than revenue
- Capital plan: seed round must reach a demonstrable milestone without project finance
- Unit economics: credible path to cost parity with the incumbent process within 5 years
- Exclusions: direct-air-capture moonshots without offtake, consumer carbon apps, offset marketplaces
- Check size: $500K to $2M; target ownership 8-15%
