# Fintech Infrastructure: Guided Thesis Answers

**What do you invest in?**
We invest in B2B financial infrastructure: payments orchestration, banking-as-a-service
middleware, compliance automation, and capital markets plumbing. Series A focus with
$2M to $8M checks. We look for companies selling to banks, fintechs, or corporates with
usage-based or transaction-based revenue models.

**What must be true about the market and traction?**
The addressable market must exceed $2B with fewer than three entrenched incumbents,
and the company must show $1M+ ARR or processed volume above $100M annually with
120%+ net revenue retention. Sales cycles longer than nine months are a concern
unless average contract value exceeds $150K.

**What are your deal breakers?**
No consumer lending, no crypto exchanges, no companies bearing balance-sheet risk,
and no teams without a licensed compliance lead or equivalent regulatory experience.
We pass on any company with a single customer representing more than 40% of revenue.
